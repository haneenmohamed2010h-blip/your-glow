YOUR GUIDE TO GLOWING SKIN

  1. FACE WASH
    • wet your face with water
    • apply a small amount
    • rinse throughly
  2. TONER
    • apply to a cotton pad
    • gently wipe your face
    • let it dry
  3. MOISTURIZER
    • apply to clean skin
    • gently massage until absorbed
  4. SUNSCREEN
    • apply before going outdoors
    • reapply every two hours when outdoors
